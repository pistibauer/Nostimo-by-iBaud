Privacy Policy

Nostimo by iBaud

Last updated: 13 October 2026

The short version. Nostimo keeps your recipes and photos on your iPhone. We have no user accounts and no servers that store your information. We don't run analytics or advertising, we don't track you, and we don't sell or share your information.

Who we are

Nostimo ("the app") is developed by iBaud ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with information on your device and what it sends over the internet to work.

Information stored on your device

The following is stored only on your iPhone, using Apple's on-device database and storage:

  • Recipes you save, write yourself or import, including their ingredients, method, notes, servings and calculated nutrition.
  • Photos you add to your own recipes, and photos of saved recipes, which are kept so recipes work offline.
  • A copy of the recipe catalog and of nutrition lookups, kept to make the app faster and to work offline.

We do not operate servers that receive, store or process this information. Like other app data, it is included in your iPhone's own backups if you use them.

Location

Location is optional. If you allow it while using the app, Nostimo uses your approximate position to find your city and country, so it can suggest dishes from your region first. Turning coordinates into a place name is done by Apple Maps, under Apple's privacy policy. Your location is not stored by the app and is never sent to us. Without location access, Nostimo uses your device's region and time zone instead.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you choose to take a photo of a dish for one of your recipes. When you pick a photo from your library, iOS gives Nostimo only the photo you selected; the app does not have access to the rest of your library. Photos are resized and stored with the recipe on your device. They are never uploaded.

Recipe search and nutrition

To show recipes, the app requests them from TheMealDB, a public recipe database. These requests contain what you search for (for example an ingredient or a dish name) and recipe identifiers. Recipe photos are loaded from the sites that host them.

To estimate nutrition for ingredients the app doesn't already know, it looks up the ingredient name in the USDA FoodData Central public nutrition database.

These requests contain only the search text, recipe identifier or ingredient name. They do not include your name, contact details, location, or any identifier that we associate with you.

Importing recipes

When you import a recipe from a link, either inside the app or with Share › Nostimo from another app, your iPhone loads that web page directly from the website it belongs to (for example a recipe site, YouTube, Facebook or a news site), much as a browser would. That website's own privacy policy applies to the visit. The page is read on your device to find the ingredients and steps; nothing is sent to us.

On iPhones that support Apple Intelligence, messy text such as a social media caption may be organised into a recipe using Apple's on-device language model. This processing happens on your iPhone.

Working with Noodle

Nostimo can work with Noodle, our calorie-tracking app, when both are installed on the same iPhone:

  • Log in Noodle passes the name, portion weight, calories and macronutrients of one serving to Noodle, where you confirm before anything is logged.
  • Nostimo shares a list of dishes (recipe names, origin, course, and per-serving calories and macronutrients) through storage on your iPhone that only our two apps can access, so you can find these dishes in Noodle.

Both happen entirely on your device. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else.

What we do not do

  • No user accounts and no sign-in.
  • No advertising and no advertising SDKs.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no data shared with data brokers.
  • No selling of personal information.

Children

Nostimo is a general cooking and recipe app. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your control over your data

Because your information is stored on your device, you control it directly. You can edit or delete any saved recipe inside the app, and deleting the app from your iPhone removes all of its stored data. Location, camera and photo permissions can be changed at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Not medical or dietary advice

Calorie and nutrition figures in Nostimo are estimates calculated from ingredient lists and public nutrition data, or taken from the recipe's publisher. They may be inaccurate. Nostimo does not provide medical or dietary advice; please consult a qualified professional before making decisions about your diet or health.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and publish the revised version here.
