Getting started

Nostimo suggests recipes for your time of day, the season and where you are, and shows the calories, protein, fat and carbohydrates per serving. There's no account to create. Just open the app.

  1. Discover shows dishes picked for right now. Swipe through them and tap one to open it.
  2. Search (the magnifying glass) finds recipes by ingredients or by dish name.
  3. Tap Save recipe to keep a recipe in Saved, where it works offline.

Frequently asked questions

How does Nostimo choose its suggestions?

It looks at the time of day on your iPhone (breakfast, lunch, dinner or later), the season, and, if you allow location, your country. Dishes from your country come first, then from nearby countries. Without location, it uses your iPhone's region setting instead. The picks change every day.

How do I search by the ingredients I have?

Open Search, choose Ingredients, and type what you have separated by commas, for example "chicken, lemon, rice". Nostimo shows the 10 recipes that use the most of them. Choose Dish name to search by name or cuisine instead. Your own saved recipes appear first, under Your recipes.

How are calories calculated?

Nostimo converts each ingredient's amount into grams and adds up the nutrition from its built-in food table, looking up anything unusual in the USDA FoodData Central database. The recipe screen tells you how many ingredients it could use, for example "Estimated from 14 of 16 ingredients", and Ingredient breakdown shows every line. When a recipe's publisher states its own nutrition, Nostimo uses that instead. Change Serves to see the figures for a different number of portions. All figures are estimates.

How do I add my own recipe with a photo?

Go to Saved, tap +, then New recipe. Add a photo with Camera or Library, then the name, servings, ingredients (one per line, amount first, e.g. "200 g spaghetti") and the method. Calories update as you type, and any ingredient Nostimo doesn't recognise is listed so you can fix it. To change a recipe later, open it and tap the pencil icon.

How do I import a recipe from a website, video or post?

In Safari, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, Apple News or another app, tap Share and choose Nostimo. Check the recipe, then tap Save to Nostimo; it appears in Saved the next time you open the app. You can also paste a link or any recipe text in Saved › + › Import from link or text.

I don't see Nostimo in the Share menu.

Tap Share, scroll the row of apps to the end and tap More, then Edit, and turn on Nostimo. You can also add it to your Favorites so it's always at the front.

An imported recipe is missing its ingredients or steps.

Some sites show the full recipe only to subscribers, and social networks such as Facebook and Instagram share only a preview of a post. If you can see the recipe, select and copy the missing part, then tap the paste icon next to Ingredients or Method on the review screen before saving. Importing the same link again later updates your saved copy.

How does Nostimo work with Noodle?

If our calorie tracker Noodle is installed, recipes show a Log in Noodle button that sends one serving's calories and macros to Noodle for you to confirm. In Noodle, the From Nostimo tile lists your Nostimo recipes and dishes. Open Nostimo once after installing so it can share its dishes. Everything stays on your iPhone.

How do I turn location on or off?

Open Settings › Nostimo › Location and choose While Using the App or Never. Nostimo works either way.

Recipes aren't loading.

Discover and Search need an internet connection the first time; after that a copy of the catalog is kept on your iPhone. Check your connection, then pull down on the Discover screen to refresh. Your saved recipes always work offline.

Where are my recipes stored? Can I get them back?

Everything is stored on your iPhone. There's no Nostimo account or cloud sync, though your recipes are included in your iPhone's own backups like other app data. Deleting the app erases its recipes. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete a recipe or my data?

In Saved, swipe a recipe to the left to delete it, or open it and tap Saved to remove it. Deleting the app removes all of its data.

Requirements

  • iPhone with iOS 26 or later
  • An internet connection for searching and importing recipes
  • Camera (optional) for photos of your own dishes
  • Apple Intelligence (optional) for tidying up messy recipe text
  • Noodle (optional) for logging recipes as meals
